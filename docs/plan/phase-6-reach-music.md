# Phase 6 — Reach & music

**Goal:** music support, more platforms, and the remaining low-priority features.
**Depends on:** Phase 4 (music needs v2 resource types).

Milestones only.

## M6.1 Music
**Goal:** local library, v2 music resources, Navidrome/Subsonic/Jellyfin/Plex music, Last.fm/ListenBrainz scrobbling, gapless/ReplayGain, mini-player ([10](../design/10-music.md), D28).
**Before starting:** answer the music questions in [10](../design/10-music.md#open-questions).

## M6.2 Hosted sync/relay (if we decide to offer one)
**Goal:** an optional hosted sync/relay. Depends on the monetization decision (D50).

## M6.3 Samsung Tizen
**Goal:** a web app client, reusing the webOS adapter approach.

## M6.4 Usenet/archive plugin
**Goal:** `nzbUrl` and archive streams (D20, low priority).
